Our audit methodology

Six areas, scored on evidence

Every audit uses the same internal 100-point diagnostic, so results are comparable over time. Each score links back to the specific pages, responses and checks behind it.

AreaPoints
AI answer visibility — mentions and citations across tested platforms25
Technical SEO and crawlability20
Service and route relevance20
Trust and business credibility15

Conversion readiness10

This is RADYNTA’s internal framework for prioritising work — not an official Google, ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity score. Areas we cannot measure are marked “not measured”, never scored as zero.

What we will never do
  • Promise rankings or AI recommendations
  • Invent reviews, ratings or certifications in your markup
  • Mass-produce thin city pages
  • Count a failed test as “not mentioned”
